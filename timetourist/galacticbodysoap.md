An advertisement break and silly jingle, written for [Time Tourist](/timetourist/). In the utopia the time travellers come from, hygiene is a public good — so the sponsor spot is performed live, in earnest, and entirely without shame. A microphone with an echo effect is set up so the actor can lean in on certain words for comic delay — *at Hub*, *the heart*, *precious*.

*Cast: an ANNOUNCER, heard but not seen. An ACTOR. STAGE HANDS.*

*A microphone with a heavy echo sits within reach of the ACTOR. On the marked words, they lean in and repeat them — delayed, booming, slightly too much.*

## A Word From Our Sponsors

**ANNOUNCER.** And now, a word from our Never-Profit Sponsors, Galactic Body Soap!

*Enter the ACTOR, nude. The STAGE HANDS flank them, holding privacy-fogged acrylic cut into cloud shapes that cover barely anything.*

**ANNOUNCER.** Reintroducing the latest in Hygiene for Being! Galactic Body Planes. Exfoliate your body by lathering with your choice of pH vitamin infused Oil, freely available *at Hub* —

*The ACTOR leans into the echo mic.* **ACTOR.** At Hub!

**ANNOUNCER.** — and dragging the Galactic Body Plane from here, to here, towards *the heart*!

*The ACTOR leans in.* **ACTOR.** The heart!

*The ACTOR does so — casually, confidently, and chastely, but with a huge beaming smile.*

**ANNOUNCER.** For sensitive areas like the belly and chest, don't let up! Press the plane in and drag to refresh your most protective and *precious* porous.

*The ACTOR leans in.* **ACTOR.** Precious!

**ANNOUNCER.** And remember —

*A jingle begins.*

> In ancient times and still today,<br>
> it really is the only way!

*An influx of bubble guns. The ACTOR exits, the STAGE HANDS and their privacy clouds following.*
