At **Lightning in a Bottle**, I worked on **Bark Kitten** as part of a hands-on immersive environment build focused on scenic fabrication, world detail, and audience-facing atmosphere.

The process included environmental dressing, repair, sign work, and iterative on-site installation across multiple themed zones.
