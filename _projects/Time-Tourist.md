[Time Tourist](https://timetourist.now/) is an annual immersive festival and game in the public service. Once a year, a group of time-travellers from a far-future utopia appear in a new town to witness a legendary historical event — and the town itself becomes the stage. Players race around to discover playable pop-ups that explain why today, of all days, is so interesting to the people of the future. The first edition lands in Fillmore, California on September 19, 2026: a free, all-ages "time travel convention" in the streets, with a ticketed multi-day track for people who want to play as Time Tourists in costume.

I am the Art Team Lead for Time Tourist, listed on the [founding creative collaborators roster](https://timetourist.now/team/). The role is to set the look of the world and run the team that builds it: visual identity, environmental design, printed matter, and the objects players actually touch. The work on this page is from that process — identity studies, cabana textile patterns, barn and barracks floorplans, and the Eden barracks rainbow-mylar interiors.
