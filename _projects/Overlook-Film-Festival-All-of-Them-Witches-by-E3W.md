All of Them Witches was an immersive game and theatrical production presented during Overlook Film Festival 2019 in New Orleans. My role included set dressing, prop fabrication, and game/puzzle component design support across the experience.

![](/assets/allofthemwitches/20190527_221621.jpg)

The project was produced by Mali Elfman and E3W Productions, written by Eva Anderson and Eric Hoff, with puzzle and path design by Tommy Honton. I collaborated on physical environment details and practical scene elements to support story beats, guest flow, and atmosphere.

![](/assets/allofthemwitches/20190527_221645.jpg)

Many of the rooms were designed to feel intimate, uncanny, and psychologically charged, with period-inspired references and carefully staged object placements.

![](/assets/allofthemwitches/20190527_221713.jpg)

The scenic language leaned heavily on portrait walls, practical lighting, weathered architectural surfaces, and hidden visual clues that rewarded close attention.

![](/assets/allofthemwitches/20190527_221728.jpg)

Character presence and environmental storytelling were tightly linked so each transition felt narratively motivated rather than purely decorative.

![](/assets/allofthemwitches/20190527_221739.jpg)

Audience movement, pacing, and reveal timing informed how props and puzzles were staged and how each room guided participants toward the next interaction.
